Welcome, we're glad you're here

The University of Massachusetts School of Law International Law Students Association

We're a group of law students who are curious about the world beyond our borders: human rights, trade, diplomacy, arbitration, and everything in between. You don't need a passport, a plan, or any experience. Just come as you are.
