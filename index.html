01 What we're about

International law can feel far away from a law school classroom. We're here to close that gap.

  1. Open doors

    We share the memberships, conferences, internships and fellowships that get students into international law, and we show you how to take the first step.

  2. Find your people

    Meetings are relaxed and welcoming. Bring your lunch, bring a friend, bring the question you were too nervous to ask in class.

  3. Hear from the field

    We bring practitioners, professors and advocates to campus, and we take requests. Tell us who you want to hear from.

    03 On the road

    October 22 to 24, 2026 / New York City

    International Law Weekend 2026

    This year's theme: [R]evolution in the International Legal Order.

    Every fall, the American Branch of the International Law Association brings practitioners, judges, diplomats, professors and students to New York for three days of panels on where international law is headed. It's the best room a law student can be in, and we go.

    Want to come with us? Talk to any board member or email ilsa.umasslaw@gmail.com.
