Get involved in international law

Not sure where to start? We built this guide for you. It covers joining the ABA, ABILA, IBA, ASIL and ILSA, finding volunteer roles, internships, fellowships, and research and writing opportunities, and bringing speakers to campus. Search it, filter it, and ask us if you get stuck.

    New to ILSA? Welcome! Work through this checklist over the semester, one small step at a time. Your progress is saved in this browser.

      Organizations to join

      Student memberships are the easiest way in. Join, then pick a committee or interest group so you start getting calls for volunteers and writers.

      Volunteer

      Conferences and competitions run on student volunteers, and it's the fastest way to meet practitioners.

      Internships & fellowships

      International positions often have deadlines six to twelve months ahead. Check each program's site for current dates and eligibility.

      Research & legal writing

      Short pieces for newsletters and blogs are a realistic first publication and a good writing sample.

      Request a speaker

      Want to hear from a practitioner, judge, diplomat, or professor? Tell the board who or what you have in mind and we'll work on bringing them to campus or online.

      • Suggest a topic even if you don't have a specific speaker.
      • Give at least four weeks of lead time when you can.
      • Mention any student group or class that might co-sponsor.
