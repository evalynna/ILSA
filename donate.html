01 Where it goes

Your gift helps us bring international law off the page and into the room.

  1. Guest speakers

    Judges, attorneys and professors who bring real-world legal insight from around the globe, followed by open Q&A and lively discussion.

  2. Cultural potlucks

    Students bring a dish from their heritage and we connect over food, culture and shared values.

  3. UN Observation Days

    Celebrations like World Peace Day and World Tourism Day that bring the global into our everyday.
